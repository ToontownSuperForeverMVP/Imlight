UmbralLessonLimits = Seeing a binding is not understanding it, and understanding is not holding it. You cannot loosen anything with your eyes. Look at a ward long enough and you will know it is there; you will not know what it guards, or why. Do not pretend otherwise.
UmbralLessonPractice = Practise gently. When you feel the pull, stop where you are and let your eyes settle; the sight comes to the patient, not the curious. And when it shows you something that belongs to someone else, look, learn, and leave it as you found it.
